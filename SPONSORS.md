## How to Sponsor

We welcome sponsors at various levels. Your sponsorship directly supports:

- Grant funding for open-source projects
- Community events and initiatives
- Infrastructure and operational costs
- Educational resources and documentation

### Sponsorship Tiers

#### 🌟 Platinum Sponsor ($10,000+/year)
- Logo placement on README.md and website (when available)
- Featured in all monthly reports
- Dedicated thank you post on social media
- Direct input on grant allocation priorities
- Quarterly meetings with the core team

#### 🥈 Gold Sponsor ($5,000+/year)
- Logo placement on SPONSORS.md and website
- Recognition in monthly reports
- Thank you post on social media
- Annual meeting with the core team

#### 🥉 Silver Sponsor ($1,000+/year)
- Name listing on SPONSORS.md
- Recognition in quarterly reports
- Thank you mention on social media

#### 💚 Community Sponsor ($100+/year)
- Name listing on SPONSORS.md
- Our eternal gratitude!

### Contact Us

To become a sponsor or learn more, please:
- Open an issue in this repository
- Email us at sponsors@newsocops.org (if available)
- Reach out via our community channels

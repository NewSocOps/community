# Grants Process

Welcome to the NewSocOps grants program! This document outlines how to apply for funding and our evaluation process.

## Overview

NewSocOps provides grants to open-source projects and initiatives that align with our mission. We support:

- **Project Development**: Core development work on open-source projects
- **Infrastructure**: Hosting, CI/CD, and operational costs
- **Documentation**: Technical writing and documentation improvements
- **Community Building**: Events, outreach, and community initiatives
- **Maintenance**: Ongoing maintenance of critical open-source projects

## Eligibility

To be eligible for a grant, your project should:

- Be open source (OSI-approved license preferred)
- Have clear goals and deliverables
- Demonstrate community value
- Show sustainable development practices
- Align with NewSocOps mission and values

## Grant Sizes

We offer grants in the following ranges:

- **Micro Grants**: $500 - $2,000 (fast-track approval)
- **Standard Grants**: $2,000 - $10,000 (standard review process)
- **Major Grants**: $10,000+ (extended review and milestones)

## Application Process

### 1. Submit Application

Use our [Grant Application Template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/grant-application.yml) to submit your proposal. Include:

- Project description and goals
- Team information and background
- Detailed budget breakdown
- Timeline and milestones
- Expected impact and outcomes
- Links to project repository and documentation

### 2. Initial Review (1-2 weeks)

Our team will:
- Review your application for completeness
- Assess alignment with our mission
- May request additional information
- Label your application appropriately

### 3. Community Discussion (1-2 weeks)

- Application is open for community feedback
- Engage constructively with questions and suggestions
- Demonstrate community support

### 4. Committee Evaluation (1 week)

Our grants committee evaluates:
- Technical merit and feasibility
- Team capability and track record
- Budget justification
- Community impact potential
- Sustainability and long-term value

### 5. Decision

You will be notified of the decision via your application issue. Possible outcomes:

- **Approved**: Grant awarded with specified conditions
- **Approved with Modifications**: Adjustments needed before funding
- **Deferred**: Needs more work, reapply in future
- **Declined**: Does not meet current criteria

### 6. Grant Agreement

If approved:
- Sign grant agreement outlining terms
- Provide payment details
- Confirm milestone schedule
- Agree to reporting requirements

### 7. Disbursement

- **Micro Grants**: Single payment upon agreement
- **Standard Grants**: 50% upfront, 50% upon completion
- **Major Grants**: Milestone-based payments (typically 3-4 payments)

### 8. Reporting

Grant recipients must:
- Submit progress updates at agreed intervals
- Provide a final report upon completion
- Acknowledge NewSocOps funding in project materials
- Report any significant changes to scope or timeline

## Tips for a Strong Application

✅ **Do:**
- Be specific about goals and deliverables
- Provide a realistic budget with justification
- Show your team's relevant experience
- Demonstrate community need and support
- Include metrics for measuring success
- Link to existing work and track record

❌ **Don't:**
- Submit vague or incomplete applications
- Overpromise on deliverables
- Ignore budget justification
- Apply for personal projects without community value
- Forget to include contact information

## FAQ

### Can I apply for multiple grants?

Yes, but each should be for a distinct project or scope of work. We typically don't fund the same work twice.

### Do I need to be a registered organization?

No, we can fund individuals, teams, or organizations. However, payment processing may vary.

### What if my project scope changes?

Contact us immediately. Significant changes may require committee re-approval.

### Can I reapply if declined?

Yes, after addressing the reasons for decline. Wait at least one grant cycle before reapplying.

### How is grant success measured?

Based on the milestones and metrics in your application. We focus on deliverables, not hours worked.

### What reporting is required?

- **Micro Grants**: One final report
- **Standard Grants**: Mid-point and final reports
- **Major Grants**: Reports at each milestone payment

### Are there restrictions on how funds are used?

Funds must be used for the purposes stated in your application. Common allowed uses:
- Developer compensation
- Infrastructure costs
- Tools and services
- Documentation and design
- Community events

Not allowed:
- Personal expenses unrelated to the project
- Hardware purchases (without prior approval)
- Lobbying or political activities
